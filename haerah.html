Can you read a drug test?

This is a 2-panel urine screen for methamphetamine (MET) and marijuana (THC), the two drugs most commonly screened in the Philippines. Run a specimen and read it like an analyst. Hint: it works the opposite way from a pregnancy test.

  1. Collect and check the specimen
  2. Drag the dropper to the sample well
  3. Wait the 5-minute read time
  4. Read and report the result
